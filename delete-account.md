# Delete your Basha Bazar account

## In the app (immediate)

Open **Basha Bazar → Profile → Delete account**.

This permanently deletes your account and all data attached to it: your to-lets and
contributions, your photos, your recorded walking routes, and your phone-number scan
history. It cannot be undone.

## By request

If you can no longer access the app, email **tasmirolislam@gmail.com** from the address
you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

| Data | Deleted |
|---|---|
| Account (email, name, Google account id) | Yes |
| To-lets and contributions you created | Yes |
| Photos you uploaded | Yes |
| Recorded walking routes | Yes |
| Phone-number scan history and scanned images | Yes |

Contributions other users added to a place you created are also removed, because
contributions are attached to the place.

See the [privacy policy](./) for full detail.
